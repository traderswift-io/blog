---
title: How were the new machines prepared?
description: Built from the first block, locked shut, and never backed up.
order: 3
---

The four new machines were prepared in three steps before they held anything.

- **Synced from the first block.** On 15 November each new machine replayed the Signer chain from the start. Its results matched the running chain's at four separate heights up to 200,000.
- **Sealed.** Sealed means locked shut: after the seal, no one can log in to a machine, including us. All four were sealed on 16 November, and they can still talk to each other and to the chain.
- **No backups.** The machines were made with backups off and no snapshot or image ever taken. So there is no copy of a share anywhere else.
