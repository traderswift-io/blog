---
title: How did we prove the new key works?
description: A refusal, an entry and a close, with real money.
order: 5
---

With real orders, on a small live account with real money.

The owner started the test with one tap, and the exchange connection was switched on only for the length of the test.

- **Refusal.** An order over the cap was refused before it reached the exchange.
- **Entry.** A small buy filled immediately.
- **Close.** A close-only sell closed it.

All three were signed by the new key.
