How was the new key made?

Four machines make one key together, in four steps.

TraderSwift update1 min readRead Markdown ↗

The new key took four steps, two of them signed by the owner.

  1. The owner added the new machines. In one signed step, the owner's wallet added the four new machines to the chain, as validators (machines that run the chain) and as signers. Until the old set left, the chain ran with eight validators, the old four and the new four.
  2. The new machines made a key together. They used joint key generation: each machine makes its own share, and the full key is never put together, on any machine, at any point. Any three of the four can sign; two cannot.
  3. The owner approved it on the exchange. The owner's account on Hyperliquid approved the new key as a new agent, with a new name.
  4. The policy moved over unchanged. The trading policy was bound to the new key with the same caps as before.
