---
title: Why not just copy the shares across?
description: Why a copied share is still the old key.
order: 2
---

Because a copied share is still the old key.

Whoever had access to the old machines would still hold a part of it.

There was a second reason. Our launch key had existed whole once, before it was split.

It was an exchange agent: a key the owner approves on the exchange so it can trade for the account. The owner had already approved it before Signer existed.

A rotation was the chance to replace it with a key made by the new machines themselves.

So the rotation does not move the old key. It makes a new one, has the owner approve it, and retires the old one.
