How were the new machines prepared?

Built from the first block, locked shut, and never backed up.

TraderSwift update1 min readRead Markdown ↗

The four new machines were prepared in three steps before they held anything.

  • Synced from the first block. On Sun 15 Nov 2026 each new machine replayed the Signer chain from the start. Its results matched the running chain's at four separate heights up to 200,000.
  • Sealed. Sealed means locked shut: after the seal, no one can log in to a machine, including us. All four were sealed on Mon 16 Nov 2026, and they can still talk to each other and to the chain.
  • No backups. The machines were made with backups off and no snapshot or image ever taken. So there is no copy of a share anywhere else.
