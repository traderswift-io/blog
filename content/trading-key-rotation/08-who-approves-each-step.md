The owner.

Since 14 November the chain's admin has been the owner's own wallet, so every change to who signs needs the owner's signature.

The rotation took two of them: one to add the new set and one to remove the old.

The new key's approval on the exchange and the start of the live test were also the owner's.

The machines and the agents prepared everything else, and the owner signed what changed control.
