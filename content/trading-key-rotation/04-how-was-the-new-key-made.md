---
title: How was the new key made?
description: Four machines make one key that never exists whole.
order: 4
---

1. **The owner added the new machines.** In one signed step, the owner's wallet added the four new machines to the chain, both as validators and as signers. Until the old set left, the chain ran with eight validators, the old four and the new four.
2. **The new machines made a key together.** The four ran a joint key generation. Each ended up with its own share, and the full key was never put together, on any machine, at any point. Any three of the four can sign; two cannot.
3. **The owner approved it on the exchange.** The owner's account on Hyperliquid approved the new key as a new agent, with a new name.
4. **The policy moved over unchanged.** The trading policy was bound to the new key with the same caps as before.
