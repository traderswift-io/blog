A rotation is where a key that "no one holds" has to prove it.

The easy way would have been to copy the shares onto new machines.

The right way was to make a new key that never existed whole, show it trading with real money, and only then take the old set away.

It also found a gap we wouldn't have seen otherwise, and the next rotation's checklist will close it. That rotation is also where Policy v2 is planned to arrive.
