TraderSwift is for three kinds of people.

- **Traders** who want an agent to trade for them, with limits the agent cannot break.
- **Builders** who want to earn from open-source front ends and apps they have made, including ones built with AI agents and AI coding tools.
- **Network participants**: anyone who provides something the network needs, such as running a validator or a data node, or who pays to use what others provide.

## How do builders get paid?

Through x402, a standard for paying inside a web request. It settles in USDC on Base.

A builder puts a price on a request.

The caller pays, and the request goes through, with full payment directly to the builder.

We never hold the money.

The payment design is accepted, and the wallet can pay. The first real payment has not been made yet.
