Each of our sites will have a blog like this one.

The blogs are for learning. They hold updates like this one, and how-to guides that walk you through doing things yourself.

## How do the guides work?

Each guide has steps, code where you need it, and prompts you can give your own AI model.

You do not have to read every line. Paste the prompt into your agent, and it walks you through.

## Can my AI read this blog?

Yes. Every page is also published as plain Markdown, and the site has a "For agents" file that lists every page.

Point your agent at it, and ask it what you want to know.

## Where is the history?

The blog is open source. Every change is a commit in Git, so you can see what was written and when.

A published post is revised in the open, never deleted.
