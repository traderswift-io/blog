Some people want to do more than use TraderSwift, or run a part of it.

They want to build a new part. We call them founders, and we would like to hear from you.

## What would a founder build?

A subnet: a chain with one job, its own validators and its own vote. [Chapter 24](24-how-does-the-network-fit-together.html) explains subnets.

It would do a job the network does not do yet.

Maybe you know a kind of market data we don't carry. Maybe you have an idea for a record that traders or agents need, and nobody keeps yet.

## How do I start?

Tell us what you want to build. [CHECK: where founders reach us]

You don't need a finished plan, or a team. An idea and the reason it matters is enough to start the conversation.

We will tell you honestly what the network can and cannot do for it today.

## How does a new subnet join?

A new subnet enters the hub only when the hub's admin adds its charter.

The admin is a multisig, so several keys must agree before that happens.

The new subnet's validators then join under the open-join rules. Those are the published rules for how new validators may join a subnet.

Today, the hub does not yet take charters from other teams. Our own first charters are still waiting, as [chapter 24](24-how-does-the-network-fit-together.html) explains.

## How do we support founders?

We want to work with founders, not just let them in.

That can go as far as a founder taking a subnet on as their own project. Its validators vote on its rules, and that vote binds.

There are no terms and no programme for this yet. We will not describe terms that do not exist.

## Do I need us at all?

No.

Every part of TraderSwift is open source. You can fork it, run it privately, or compete with us.

[Chapter 03](03-why-is-traderswift-open-source.html) explains why we think that is the bare minimum.

More people building on the code, and hardening it, helps everyone who uses it. That includes a founder who starts with us and later goes their own way.

## What is live?

**Planned.** The hub does not yet take charters from other teams.
