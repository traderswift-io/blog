Ledger and Vault are tools for you, not only for your agents.

**Ledger** holds your tasks and your decisions. **Vault** holds your notes and your files.

You can use them yourself, every day, with no agent at all. When you do want an agent to help, you let it read the part it needs, and nothing more.

## What would I use Ledger for?

Ledger keeps what you need to do, and what you need to decide.

Its items are tasks and cards. A card is a question waiting for your answer.

### How do I keep a task I set myself?

Say you want to check the funding rate on a market before the weekend.

You add it to Ledger as a task. It stays there until you mark it done, and nobody else sees it.

### How do I record a decision?

Say you decide to stop trading one market for a month, because it keeps hitting your stops.

You record the decision and the reason in Ledger. A month later, you can look back and see exactly what you decided, and why.

## What would I use Vault for?

Vault keeps what you write and what you save.

### How do I keep a note?

Say you write a short note each morning: what the market did overnight, and what you are watching today.

Each note goes into Vault. Over weeks, those notes become your own record of how you read the market.

### How do I keep a file?

Say you save a chart screenshot, a broker statement or a research paper.

The file goes into Vault, locked the same way as your notes.

## Can they work together?

Yes. Ledger and Vault are two separate tools, but they share one way of linking.

A task can show up inside a Vault project, and a Vault project can be linked from inside a task. You decide which links to make.

The links are kept inside your encrypted data, so they stay as private as the rest.

## Why does having them on TraderSwift help me?

- **Your data is private by default** – it is encrypted on your phone, and nothing leaves the device unless you choose.
- **No company holds your data** – that includes us, so nobody can read it, lock you out or close it down.
- **Agents read only what you grant** – you can let one agent read your trading notes, and not your personal tasks.
- **You choose the screen** – if you stop liking one app, you switch to another, and your data stays where it is.
- **One home for all your work** – your tasks, notes and trade ideas all sit on TraderSwift, so the tools can work together.

That last point matters for TraderSwift AI. The more TraderSwift tools you use, the better TraderSwift AI knows you, as [chapter 22](22-what-is-traderswift-ai.html) explains.

## Who makes the screens?

We build what sits underneath: the place your data is kept, and the rules for who may read it.

The screens you look at are called front ends, and anyone can build one. You choose the front end you want inside your TraderSwift wallet.

If a builder charges for their front end, you pay them directly. [Chapter 20](20-what-is-the-marketplace-and-how-do-builders-get-paid.html) explains how.

## Why are they on a blockchain?

A blockchain is a shared record kept by several machines, where no single machine can change the past on its own.

Ledger and Vault each have their own, called `ledger-1` and `vault-1`. Each one answers two questions only:

1. Where is your data?
2. Who may read it?

Your data itself never goes on a chain: not your tasks, not your notes, not your files. The chain holds the map and the permissions, and the data stays where you put it, encrypted.

That means no company holds the master list of who may read what, including us. [Chapter 18](18-where-is-my-data-kept-and-who-can-read-it.html) explains where the data goes and how you share it.

## What is live?

- **Built, not live** – the two chains. Each passed a full round trip on one test machine on Wed 18 Nov 2026, and neither is on a public network yet.
- **Built, not live** – the encrypted store on your phone, and the screen where you choose where your data is kept. Both were merged on Wed 18 Nov 2026, and neither has been used on a real phone yet.
- **Planned** – recovery if you lose your phone. Until it is built, losing your phone loses the items saved on it.
