title: What is next?
description: What each part of the system needs next.
order: 11

- **Hub.** Finish the admin handover, then register the front-end and storage charters.
- **Signer.** Policy v2 brings a price band, a maximum distance from the exchange's mark price on every order, checked by each signer on its own price feed. It also brings a cap on how many orders a key can sign per second. It needs another rotation before outside signers can join.
- **Storage.** Bring up the founding providers, then the security review before any real data.
- **Wallet and payments.** Prove ring ownership across chains, and finish the x402 payment path.
- **The rest.** Choose the Bus core, finish moving apps onto the gate, then DNS.
- **Servers.** Finish the move to Proxmox, and prove the rollback before retiring the old setup.
