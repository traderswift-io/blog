---
title: What is the wallet, and why does it have no seed phrase?
description: One core, three skins, and a device ring instead of a seed phrase.
order: 5
---

The TraderSwift wallet is one shared core with interchangeable skins.

The core owns every signing screen and the approval text, so a skin can change how it looks but never what you approve.

We have three skins: Glass, Orbit and Night Key. Every skin keeps text contrast at 4.5:1 or better.

![The wallet's first screen, on a phone](../assets/since-lockbox/wallet-signin-390-light.png "The first screen a visitor sees: no setup, no seed phrase.")

Two design ideas set it apart:
- **Door-first.** You are inside the wallet before any setup. Looking around needs nothing; you create a key only at the first action that needs one.
- **The device ring.** Instead of a seed phrase, your account is owned by your own devices, any two of three. One phone alone is never enough. If you lose everything, recovery takes seven days by default, never less than three, and any of your enrolled devices can cancel it.

The ring is a design that has been accepted; the first screens are built.

On 17 November the Signer approval screen passed its second review and was merged.
