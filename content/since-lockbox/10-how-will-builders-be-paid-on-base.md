People who build front ends on TraderSwift will be paid directly by the people who use them.

[What is the marketplace, and how do builders get paid?](../what-is-traderswift/20-what-is-the-marketplace-and-how-do-builders-get-paid.html) explains how payment works.

This week, the wallet's payment part was built. It passed its checks against test data.

Payments are **Built, not live**: some pieces they need are not ready yet, and no real payment has been made.
