We also studied other people's open work, and planned one piece of our own.

## What is Goose?

Goose is an open-source agent from Block.

We plan to host it unchanged before we consider any fork. It is **Planned**.

The same survey of Block's open-source work also points us at Buzz, Bitkey's recovery and Bluetooth experiments.

## Why run our own DNS?

DNS is the system that turns a web address into the machine that answers it.

We have a design for running our own, with independent secondaries and a staged rollback. It is **Planned**.

It waits on the sign-in gate going live.
