Each part has a next step, with a date where we have one.

## What is next for Signer?

- **Fri 20 Nov 2026.** The next rotation, with Policy v2 and two new venues, Aster and Base.
- **Policy v2.** New limits: a price band, so no order strays too far from the exchange's reference price, and a cap on how many orders a key can sign per second. It is **Built, not live** and in review.
- **Sat 21 Nov 2026.** Signer for your own Hyperliquid account. It waits on the rotation, an audit decision by the owner, and a live test with a new user.

## What is next for the hub?

- **First charters.** Register the first subnets, for front ends and Storage. There is no date: they wait on one security step, then the owner's approval.

## What is next for Storage?

- **Founding providers.** Bring them up, then the outside review of the encryption, before any real data.

## What is next for the wallet and payments?

- **Device ring.** Build its screens.
- **Payments.** Take the payment part live once the pieces it waits on are ready.

## What else is next?

- **Bus.** Build the parts that are designed, such as encrypted channels, then deploy it.
- **Sign-in.** Move apps onto the gate, then DNS.
- **Servers.** Finish the move to Proxmox, and prove the rollback before retiring the old setup.
