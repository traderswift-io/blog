Signer holds a trading key in shares across several machines, and signs an order only when a quorum agrees it fits the owner's policy on the chain.

These pages show you how to use it: what it is, how to try it, how to write a policy, how to run a validator, and what is coming.

Every page has steps you can follow and a prompt you can give your own AI agent.

Pages marked **Planned** describe work that isn't finished yet. Each says what exists today, what we plan, and the milestones.

When the work is done, the page is updated.

One thing to know first: Signer is live on Hyperliquid, but only for our own accounts.

Outsiders can't get a key for their own account until the next signer rotation.

You can run a validator, run your own Signer chain, and learn every part of it today.
