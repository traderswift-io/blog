---
title: How will we teach it?
description: What this blog is for, and how to use it with your own AI.
---

Each of our sites will have a blog like this one.

The blogs are for learning: updates, and how-tos that walk you through doing things yourself.

Each how-to has steps, code where you need it, and prompts you can give your own AI. Copy the prompt into your agent, and it walks you through.

Every page is also published as plain Markdown. The site has a "For agents" file that lists every page, so you can point your agent at it and ask what you want to know.

The blog is open source. Every change is a commit in Git, so you can see what was written and when.

A published post is revised in the open, never deleted.
