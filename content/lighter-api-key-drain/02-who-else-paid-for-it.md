---
title: Who else paid for it?
description: One account's key leaked, and every trader on the exchange was locked in.
order: 2
---

Every other trader on Lighter.

One account's key leaked, and the response was to pause withdrawals for all users.

At 12:49 Lighter said its target was to resume by the end of the day ([Lighter](https://x.com/Lighter_xyz/status/2122311006604984497)). Withdrawals came back about seven hours after the pause ([CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/lighter-withdrawals-resume-leaked-api-keys)).

Upbit suspended deposits and withdrawals of Lighter's token too ([Upbit](https://upbit.com/service_center/notice?id=1ae2de21-f9f8-45a1-bc74-8d2758ca6167)).

The halt came after the first $10.4 million had already left. It could not undo that.

It only locked everyone else in. When the only control is at the exchange, the exchange has one lever, and it pulls it on everyone.

A policy at the key is a control at the source. It limits what one account's key can do, so a leak stays one account's problem.
