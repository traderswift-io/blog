Who is TraderSwift for?

The four kinds of people TraderSwift serves: traders, builders, network participants and founders.

TraderSwift update2 min readRead Markdown ↗

TraderSwift is for four kinds of people.

  • Traders – who want an agent to trade for them, safely.
  • Builders – who make apps and front ends for traders.
  • Network participants – who run part of the network, or pay to use it.
  • Founders – who want to build a chain of their own on TraderSwift.

Each one is below.

What does a trader get?

An agent that trades for you, with limits it cannot break.

The agent trades through Signer, which never signs a transfer or a withdrawal. You can stop the agent with one signed message.

You also get your own tools: Ledger for your tasks and decisions, Vault for your notes and files. You can use them with an agent, or on your own.

Signer is Live on our own account. For your own Hyperliquid account it is Built, not live, with a target of no earlier than Sun 22 Nov 2026.

Ledger and Vault are Built, not live. Chapter 08 explains the key, and chapter 16 explains the tools.

What does a builder get?

Builders earn from open-source front ends and apps they've made with AI agents and AI coding tools.

They are paid in full, directly. Chapter 20 explains how.

Who are network participants?

They are people who run part of the network, such as a validator or a data node, and people who pay to use what others run.

A validator is one of the machines that keeps a chain running and checks what goes on it. A data node is a machine that serves data, such as market prices.

Chapter 24 explains how the network fits together.

Who are founders?

Founders are people who want to build one of our subnet chains.

A subnet is a chain of its own that joins the TraderSwift network, with its own job, such as storage or signals.

We support founders and work with them. That can go as far as a founder taking a subnet on as their own project.
