Why not just use the tools I have today?

Task apps, note apps and file stores already exist. Here is what they cost you, and what changes when no company holds your data.

TraderSwift update4 min readRead Markdown ↗

Good tools for tasks, notes and files already exist.

Millions of people use them every day, and many of them work well. So why build new ones?

What does a tool run by one company cost me?

Most of today's tools are centralized. That means one company runs them, on that company's computers.

You pay for that in three ways, even when the tool is free.

Who can read my data?

Your tasks, notes and files sit on the company's computers, not yours.

Most of these apps can read what you store. Their staff, their systems, and anyone who breaks into them can get to it.

When one company holds everyone's data, a single break-in exposes everyone at once.

What if the company changes its terms?

The company sets the price and the rules, and it can change them.

It can charge more, limit what a free account can do, or change what it does with your data. You either accept, or you leave and move everything yourself.

What if the company closes?

A company can shut a service down, or close your account.

When it does, your data goes with it, unless you got it out in time.

Why does this matter more for a trader?

A trader's notes are not a shopping list.

Your tasks, your research and your trade notes describe how you trade. Chapter 13 explains why a leak of your trade logs can give your strategy away, and your notes can do the same.

So the company that holds your notes holds a map of your edge.

What does TraderSwift do differently?

Each cost above has an answer.

  • Nobody else holds your data – it is encrypted on your phone first, and by default it never leaves the device. No company holds the master copy, including us.
  • No single company sets the rules – who may read your data is recorded on a chain, not in one company's database.
  • Nothing closes with a company – your data stays on your phone, and if one app closes, you switch to another without moving anything.
  • Agents read only what you grant – nothing is shared by default.

Should I switch today?

Not yet.

Ledger and Vault are built, not live. Recovery after a lost phone is not built either, so losing your phone today would lose the items saved on it.
