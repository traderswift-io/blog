Anyone can say their agent is good.

Reviews today are mostly stars, comments and screenshots. They are easy to fake, and hard to check.

Our answer, in one line: Signer creates a verification layer for our agentic review system, used in our planned marketplace.

## What does that mean in plain words?

A verification layer is something that checks a claim against what really happened.

The agentic review system is how agents in our marketplace will be reviewed. [Chapter 20](20-what-is-the-marketplace-and-how-do-builders-get-paid.html) explains the marketplace.

Signer is the part of TraderSwift that signs an agent's orders, inside limits its owner sets. [Chapter 09](09-how-does-an-order-get-signed-and-how-do-i-stop-it.html) explains it.

## Why does Signer make reviews honest?

An agent that trades through Signer cannot sign anything on its own.

Every order request it makes is recorded, whether it was signed or refused. [Chapter 10](10-how-would-i-know-if-someone-tried-to-break-in.html) explains that record.

The agent's maker does not write that record, and cannot edit it later. So a review of an agent can rest on what the agent actually did, not on what its maker says it does.

## What does this mean when I choose an agent?

Today, choosing a trading agent is mostly a leap of faith.

You read a sales page, look at a chart someone made, and hope. If the agent breaks its own rules, you usually find out with your money.

A review that rests on Signer's record changes the question you ask. It's no longer "do I believe this maker?", but "what did this agent actually do?"

- **Did it stay inside its limits?** Every request was either signed inside the limits, or refused.
- **How often was it refused?** An agent that keeps asking for orders outside its limits is telling you something.
- **Has it run for long?** A record that covers weeks says more than one that covers a day.

So you can pick an agent on how it behaved, not on how well it was marketed.

## Why does this help a builder?

If you build an agent, good behaviour builds its reputation for you.

You don't need a big name or a marketing budget. An agent that keeps to its rules can show it.

## What stays private?

The review layer uses the same record that Signer already keeps.

[Chapter 11](11-what-can-other-people-see-on-the-chain.html) explains exactly what that record shows, and what it never shows.

- **Live** – Signer's record of each request, on Hyperliquid, since 14 Nov 2026.
- **Planned** – the agentic review system.
- **Built, not live** – the marketplace it will be used in.

Exactly what a review will show is still being designed. The points above are the questions it is meant to answer, not a finished design.
