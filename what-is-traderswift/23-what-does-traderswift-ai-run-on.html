What does TraderSwift AI run on?

The computers that will run TraderSwift AI, where they start, and where they are meant to go.

TraderSwift update2 min readRead Markdown ↗

An AI needs computers to learn and to answer.

The computers that train and serve TraderSwift AI are called its compute. Our line on it is simple: it starts on our own machines beside our nodes; opening it to others is the aim.

What is a node?

A node is a computer that runs part of the TraderSwift network.

Some nodes keep a chain's record. Others serve data, such as the charts data node in chapter 14.

Why beside our nodes?

The AI works with what the network holds, so it sits next to the network.

It works with your Ledger and Vault, and reads them only through the grants you give it. Chapter 22 explains those grants.

Is it decentralized?

Not at the start.

Decentralized means many independent people run it, not one. For the AI's compute, that is the aim, not the launch.

We would rather say that plainly now than claim it early.

Can I provide compute?

Not yet.

There is no way today for anyone else to provide compute for the AI, and no design for it yet.

When that changes, it will appear here, and in chapter 24, with the other ways to take part in the network.

What is not decided yet?

We have not published machine sizes, costs or locations.

We will add them here when they are settled.

What is live?

Planned.
